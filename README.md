# Mini ERP Database & Business Analysis — TechStore

## Project Overview
This project simulates a small retail business database using SQLite. It focuses on relational database design, data management, and SQL-based business analysis to support business decision-making.

## Business Scenario
TechStore is a fictional electronics retailer that sells products such as laptops, smartphones, accessories, and tablets. The database was designed to manage customers, products, sales orders, order details, and inventory.

## Database Structure
The database consists of five main tables:

- **Customers** – Customer information and registration details.
- **Products** – Product catalog, categories, prices, and suppliers.
- **Orders** – Customer orders and order status.
- **Order_Items** – Products included in each order, quantities, and transaction prices.
- **Inventory** – Current stock levels and reorder thresholds.

The database uses primary keys, foreign keys, and relational constraints to maintain data consistency.

## Business Analysis
15 SQL queries were developed to analyze the business, including:

- Product and category revenue analysis
- Best-selling products
- Customer spending analysis
- Order status monitoring
- Inventory and restocking analysis
- Customer loyalty and repeat purchases
- Sales performance by city
- Monthly revenue analysis
- High-value orders
- Inventory valuation
